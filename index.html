Gastronomy & Drinks

At Villa Anežka, food is one of the main reasons to come here. We cook the food we truly love — a mix of Bohemian tradition, Mediterranean simplicity, and Eastern European comfort, always with a focus on local ingredients and beautiful presentation.

The menu follows the seasons: trout and pike-perch, crispy duck, grilled steaks, farmhouse cheeses, young vegetables, homemade dumplings and kulajda. Every dish is built around the ingredients.

We work with local farmers, cheesemakers, butchers, and small producers from the region. Fresh vegetables, artisan cheeses, carefully selected meat, and local fish give our kitchen its honest and distinctive character.

Drinks are just as important. We serve wines from the best Moravian wineries and beers from renowned Žatec and local craft brewers, each chosen to pair naturally with the food at Villa Anežka.
